2026-07-29
🚀 GTC-2.5 & GTC-2.5 mini Released — The Foundation is Set
We are thrilled to announce the release of GTC-2.5 , our most capable production-ready foundation model to date, alongside GTC-2.5 mini , a lightweight open-source variant for the community. At 400 million parameters , GTC-2.5 represents a significant scale-up from our earlier 64M Preview models. Trained on a curated 19GB dataset combining high-quality Chinese and English text, code, and mathematical content, it delivers substantially improved coherence, knowledge retention, and instruction-following capabilities. GTC-2.5 mini (64M parameters) is now available under a permissive license on Hugging Face and GitHub — perfect for research, experimentation, and lightweight edge applications. This release marks the end of the "Preview" era for GTC and the beginning of a more mature, product-oriented phase. 📖

2026-07-29
🔄 GTC-2.5o Preview — Expanding the Horizon with Full-Modal Understanding
We are excited to introduce GTC-2.5o , the first truly multimodal member of the GTC family. Built on the GTC-2.5 foundation, GTC-2.5o extends with full multimodal capabilities — text, image, and audio understanding — in a unified model. Integrating vision and audio encoders directly into the 64M-parameter backbone, GTC-2.5o supports image captioning, visual question answering, audio comprehension, and cross-modal reasoning. It validates TAI Research's multimodal pipeline and serves as a baseline for larger vision-language-audio models. The model is currently in early preview, available to Pro users on Sigma. We are actively collecting feedback and plan to iterate rapidly. 📖

2026-07-14
🖼️ GTC-2.5V Preview Released — TAI Research's First Vision-Language Model
We are excited to announce the release of GTC-2.5V Preview, the first vision-language foundation model developed by TAI Research. Extending the GTC series with multimodal capabilities, this 64M-parameter model integrates a lightweight visual encoder with a decoder-only language backbone, trained end-to-end on a V100 32G GPU. GTC-2.5V Preview demonstrates foundational capabilities in image captioning, visual question answering, and text reading from images. While it is a compact research preview, it serves as a baseline for our upcoming larger vision-language models and downstream applications, including multimodal agents and Computer Use scenarios. This release reaffirms our commitment to transparent, efficient AI research across modalities. We extend our gratitude to the MiniMind-V team for their open-source contributions, enabling us to explore multimodal AI efficiently. 📄 System Card Available: Download GTC-2.5V Preview System Card (PDF)
