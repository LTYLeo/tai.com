A Strategic Shift: From Model Iteration to Foundational Research

2026-09-15 · TAI Research

TAI Research was founded on a simple belief: intelligence should be accessible, and innovation should not be limited by age or scale. Over the past period, we have built, trained, and released a series of models under the GTC name. These models validated our training pipeline, taught us how to ship products, and gave us our first real users. They served their purpose.

But we have also learned something important along the way. Building better base models, in the current landscape, requires resources that far exceed what a small research team can reasonably command. Compute, data, and talent are concentrated in the hands of a few. Competing on their terms means running a race we cannot win—and more importantly, a race that is not ours to run.

So we are making a deliberate shift.

GTC Series: Maintenance Mode

The GTC series will continue to exist, but it will no longer be our primary focus. We may release occasional small updates, but we will not open new version numbers. The series has done its job. It proved we could build. Now it is time to do something harder.

TFMF: A New Model Line

Going forward, our model products will be organized under the TFMF series. Unlike GTC, TFMF is not about building base models from scratch. It is about taking mature open-source foundations and making them better—through post-training, adaptation, and product integration. TFMF will evolve in generations: TFMF, TFMF 2, TFMF 3, and so on. Each generation represents a step forward in capability, efficiency, and usefulness, not a reinvention of the wheel.

Our Real Focus: Long-Term Foundational Research

The decision to step back from base model competition allows us to step forward into something more meaningful. We are redirecting our core energy toward long-term foundational research—areas that are not yet crowded, not yet solved, and not yet fully understood. These are projects that require patience, deep thinking, and a willingness to explore without immediate reward. They are the reason TAI Research exists.

We cannot share the details of these research directions yet. What we can say is this: they are aimed at questions that matter, and they will take time. We are not looking for quick wins. We are looking for the next paradigm.

What This Means for Our Users

If you use our products, little changes today. The models you rely on remain available. The platforms you use continue to work. What changes is where our energy goes next—toward the deeper work that will eventually produce the next generation of intelligence, and the products built on top of it.

A Note on Why We Are Doing This

It is tempting, in this field, to chase benchmarks, to ship models, to optimize for attention. We have done some of that. But attention is not the same as progress. And progress, real progress, comes from asking questions that others are not yet asking—and being willing to spend years looking for answers.

That is what we are choosing to do.

We are a small team. We do not have unlimited resources. But we have something that matters: the freedom to choose our own path, and the patience to walk it.

Thank you for being with us.

— TAI Research
