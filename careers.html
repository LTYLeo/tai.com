🚀 Core Team Hiring

Find the people building the next generation of intelligence

Who we are

TAI Research is an AI research team founded by high-school students and run to top-lab standards. We have no billion-dollar budget and no ten-thousand-GPU cluster. What we do have is sharp algorithmic intuition and a track record of running frontier architectures on a few dollars of cloud compute — including the long-video closed-loop generation and emotional-companion models we recently completed.

The reality we work in

Compute is limited and time is fragmented, so we need people with strong self-drive who can break a problem open on their own. If you would rather not be another tuning cog in a big company, if you want to help define an AI product's commercial loop from zero to one, or push the time limits of low-level intelligence, come join us.

💼 Open Roles

Full-time / part-time / remote-flexible

AI Product & Full-Stack Engineer AI Product & Full-Stack

You will: Own end-to-end development of TAI Teens across mobile and web: wire the model inference API, the billing system (payments / redeem codes) and the interface into one working loop.

We are looking for:

  • Fluent in at least one backend language (Python / Node.js) and a frontend framework (React / Vue)
  • Have shipped a complete consumer product on your own
  • Can stay calm and fix things fast when a server falls over or a payment callback fails

Alignment & Data Strategy Researcher Alignment & Data

You will: Design the high-quality dialogue data pipeline and lead SFT and LoRA finetuning. You own safety guardrails and persona alignment, and the long-context problems of logical idling and forgetting.

We are looking for:

  • Know the post-training stack (SFT / DPO / RLHF) and tools like HuggingFace / PEFT
  • Extremely sensitive to data: can spot template collapse from a few hundred conversations
  • Can script automated cleaning and synthetic data generation

AI Systems & Inference Engineer Systems & Inference

You will: Build and maintain our inference infrastructure. Quantise 4B-class models (INT8 / INT4), speed up inference (vLLM / llama.cpp / MLX), and find efficient ways to run on Mac and on-device.

We are looking for:

  • Understand computer architecture and the characteristics of GPU / Apple Silicon
  • CUDA or Metal experience is a strong plus
  • Have worked with elastic compute scheduling on a cloud platform

Multimodal Video / Audio Researcher Multimodal Video / Audio

You will: Explore the next generation of joint video and audio generation, and solve copy degeneration and closed-loop state explosion in long-horizon video.

We are looking for:

  • Deep understanding of diffusion models, autoregressive video generation and world models
  • Comfortable with optical flow, event alignment and continuous state estimation
  • Can validate architectural ideas with small synthetic datasets under tight compute

Post-Training Engineer Post-Training

You will: Squeeze maximum capability out of open bases like Qwen and Llama through extreme data refinement, and find the engineering path from 1B–4B models toward large-model quality.

We are looking for:

  • Deep knowledge of LoRA / QLoRA and hands-on fixes for catastrophic forgetting and overfitting
  • Understand model limits and can tailor training to a target task (code, reasoning)
  • Obsessive about compute cost: best convergence for the smallest experiment

Frontier Researcher Frontier Research

You will: Look past the current scaling laws for the next paradigm: continual learning, self-improving models, recurrent architectures, neuromorphic computing.

We are looking for:

  • Strong critical thinking and the nerve to question mainstream deep learning
  • Solid grounding in mathematics, physics or cognitive science
  • Willing to take big research risk and stay on hard problems for a long time
  • If you have a self-built, ambitious, still-imperfect experimental project, that is exactly what we want

🌟 The Wild Card

The Wild Card
